## Story overview

Anuj Dhanuka is a software engineer and frontend developer whose path into technology combines engineering discipline, practical business ownership, continuous learning, design mentorship and production product work.

He was born in Indore. When he was around ten years old, his family moved to Cheepurupalli, a small town in the Vizianagaram district of Andhra Pradesh, where his family continues to live.

During the final year of his B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering, Anuj became interested in software and began exploring how applications were built. His regular coursework ended in 2019, with two subjects still remaining. He joined an institute where he learned computer fundamentals, Java and Android Studio and completed that training in August 2019. The course gave him his first structured understanding of programming and encouraged him to continue learning independently, beginning with HTML and CSS. He later cleared the two remaining backlogs and formally completed his B.Tech in May 2023.

## Learning ownership through the family business

After finishing his regular B.Tech coursework, Anuj also helped run his father's agro-commodities trading business. The business purchased agricultural crops such as maize, pulses and ragi and supplied them to exporters, mills and other companies.

His role extended across the day-to-day operation. He communicated with vendors and suppliers, coordinated with transport offices to move goods, managed labour and helped oversee work from procurement through delivery. This experience taught him how a business operates beyond a single task: how to take ownership, communicate with different stakeholders, solve practical problems and remain accountable for an outcome.

The COVID-19 period and unexpected financial losses affected the business significantly. Working through that period strengthened his resilience and gave him an understanding of responsibility under uncertainty. Those lessons continue to influence how he approaches product work, delivery and collaboration.

## Building a structured software foundation

While contributing to the family business, Anuj joined NxtWave's CCBP program in October 2020 to build a stronger software foundation. Through CCBP, he learned HTML, CSS, JavaScript, responsive web development, React.js, Node.js, databases and developer foundations.

He applied this learning through projects such as a Todo application, travel website, food website and Jobby application. The combination of structured lessons and project work helped him move from basic experimentation to understanding how complete web applications are planned and built.

## First professional mobile development experience

At the end of 2023 and beginning of 2024, Anuj actively searched for his first software opportunity and attended multiple interviews. He earned a two-month onsite internship with 3rd Eye Lab in Hyderabad, from April to June 2024. Moving from the Visakhapatnam region to Hyderabad for the role was an important step in committing fully to a software career.

During the internship, he applied his CCBP learning while working with React Native CLI, Firebase and Git. He developed the Levels App, a mobile quiz application with category-based questions and dynamic content. He completed the assigned work ahead of schedule and delivered additional features beyond the initial requirements.

To deepen his mobile development knowledge after the internship, he completed a dedicated React Native course on Udemy in September 2024:

## Developing design judgment and delivery breadth

In October 2024, Anuj joined PPCROY TECH SOLUTIONS as a Junior Web Developer. This role expanded his skills beyond implementation and helped him develop a stronger understanding of UI/UX principles, visual hierarchy, typography, brand guidelines, responsive design and the structure required for production-quality websites.

Anjaneyulu Jadda, founder of PPCROY TECH SOLUTIONS, became an important mentor. His guidance helped Anuj learn how to distinguish effective design from weak design, approach a project strategically, decide which sections a website needs and connect typography, branding and usability with business goals.

At PPCROY, Anuj worked across React, WordPress, Webflow and Shopify. He learned Figma, handled multiple projects at the same time and delivered more than 20 client projects across different domains. The company valued his work, and the experience strengthened both his delivery discipline and his ability to adapt to different industries, audiences and platforms.

He also learned how to use AI-assisted tools in day-to-day work and how to keep up with emerging technology. These tools included Lovable, v0, Bolt, Cursor and ChatGPT. He uses them to accelerate exploration and execution while continuing to apply his own judgment to design, implementation and quality.

## Moving into product engineering

In December 2025, Anuj joined ChefKart, a product-based company, as an Associate Software Engineer. He made this move to deepen his technical understanding, strengthen his coding skills and learn how a product evolves over time.

At ChefKart, he contributes across four product surfaces:

- The customer-facing mobile application built with React Native.
- The public website built with React and Next.js.
- The internal operations dashboard built with React.
- A payment platform built with React.

He has launched multiple features and services and collaborates with backend engineers, designers and product managers from implementation through release. His manager is also the company's co-founder and CTO, which gives him direct access to product and technical guidance. He can share ideas and suggestions directly, receive quick feedback and help move approved improvements forward efficiently.

In May 2026, his contribution was recognised through a **Technology & Engineering** appreciation at ChefKart.

## How the journey shapes his work

Each stage contributed a different part of Anuj's current approach:

- Mechanical engineering developed structured thinking and problem-solving discipline.
- The family business taught ownership, stakeholder communication and end-to-end responsibility.
- CCBP provided a structured software foundation and consistent project practice.
- 3rd Eye Lab introduced professional mobile development, Git and onsite collaboration.
- PPCROY developed his design judgment, delivery range, mentorship experience and ability to manage multiple client projects.
- ChefKart is strengthening his product thinking, technical depth and cross-functional collaboration on live software used by customers and internal teams.

This combination allows him to consider more than the interface alone. He understands that a product must serve users, support a business, communicate clearly and remain practical for the team responsible for maintaining it.

Anuj plans to continue growing as a product-minded software engineer. Alongside deepening his frontend and mobile expertise, he wants to develop practical skills in backend engineering, DevOps, artificial intelligence and machine learning. His goal is to understand more of the complete product system and contribute with greater technical range over time.
